# Epic: Financial Management

## Purpose

Enable individuals and families to manage and understand their financial reality: income, expenses, savings, investments, budgets, financial objectives, shared financial contexts, and future financial commitments.

The Epic groups the capabilities required to maintain a reliable history of financial activity and to support informed financial decisions.

## Principles

* Financial reality first: represent what economically happened, not merely cash movements.
* Historical financial facts must remain distinguishable from forecasts and future commitments.
* Shared financial context does not imply shared economic ownership.

## Completion

The Epic evolves incrementally. A Feature is complete when its approved User Stories satisfy their Acceptance Criteria and BDD scenarios and the resulting behavior is validated against the intended business need.
