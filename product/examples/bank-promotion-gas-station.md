# Example: Bank Promotion for a Gas Station Store

A snapshot of a bank promotion, as shown on the Bank's benefits page in October 2026. Terms can change or end. See the [examples index](README.md) for the rules of these files.

## Terms

| Term                                      | Value                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discount rate (*porcentaje de descuento*) | 25%                                                                  |
| Refund cap (*tope de reintegro*)          | 10,000 per month                                                     |
| Minimum purchase                          | None                                                                 |
| Valid days                                | Every day                                                            |
| Validity                                  | 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31                                             |
| Prerequisite                              | The customer is subscribed to the Bank's subscription program        |
| Payment channel                           | Only through the merchant's app                                      |
| Payment instrument                        | Debit card or credit card of the Bank, from one card network         |
| Store                                     | Only the convenience store of the gas station chain, not the fuel    |
| Refund                                    | Paid into the customer's bank account within 30 days of the purchase |

Rules in the terms and conditions:

* Valid in the participating stores, paying only through the merchant's app, with a debit or credit card of the Bank.
* Purchases paid by transfer, with money in account, through virtual wallets, or financed in installments are excluded.
* It cannot be combined with other promotions.
* Only for the holder of a bank account at the Bank. Customers with a credit card and no associated bank account are excluded.
* If an additional card is used, the refund goes to the account of the holder.
* The monthly cap adds up the purchases of the holder with the holder's cards.
* Example in the terms: a credit card purchase of 50,000 through the app gives a refund of 10,000 (the monthly cap).

## Matching example

The [Gas station receipt](gas-station-receipt.md) (14,000.00, through the merchant's app) gives a refund of 3,500.00.

## Illustrates

* A promotion is a rule. A reduction (here, a refund) is what the rule produces for one expense.
* The cap depends on other purchases of the same month.
* A promotion names a brand and a store. The receipt names a legal entity.
