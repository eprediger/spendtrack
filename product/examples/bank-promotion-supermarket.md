# Example: Bank Promotion for a Supermarket

A snapshot of a bank promotion, as shown on the Bank's benefits page in October 2026. Terms can change or end. See the [examples index](README.md) for the rules of these files.

## Terms

| Term | Value |
| ---- | ----- |
| Discount rate (*porcentaje de descuento*) | 20% |
| Refund cap (*tope de reintegro*) | 20,000 per month |
| Minimum purchase | 35,000 (the terms say "greater than or equal to") |
| Valid days | Friday and Saturday |
| Validity | 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31 |
| Prerequisite | The customer is a holder of a bank account at the Bank |
| Payment channel | A QR payment, through the Bank's app or through a QR wallet app |
| Payment instrument | Debit or credit cards of the Bank, from several card networks |
| Store | All branches of the supermarket chain, in person and online |
| Refund | Paid into the customer's bank account within 30 days of the purchase |

Rules in the terms and conditions:

* Valid in the participating stores, paying with a QR through the Bank's app or the QR wallet app, with a credit or debit card of the Bank.
* Purchases paid by transfer, with money in account, through virtual wallets, or financed in installments are excluded.
* It cannot be combined with other promotions.
* Only for the holder of a bank account at the Bank. Customers with a credit card and no associated bank account are excluded.
* If an additional card is used, the refund goes to the account of the holder.
* The monthly cap adds up the purchases with the credit card of the holder (and its additional cards) and with all the debit cards of the holder.
* Example in the terms: a credit card purchase of 100,000 gives a refund of 20,000.
