# Product Vision

## Vision

Build a financial management product that helps individuals and families understand their financial reality, make informed decisions, and work toward short-, medium-, and long-term objectives.

## Core Capabilities

The product is intended to support:

* income management;
* expense management;
* savings management;
* investment management;
* budgeting;
* financial objectives;
* shared financial contexts;
* future financial commitments;
* financial analysis and comparison.

## User Value

The product should help users:

* understand where their money is going;
* maintain a reliable history of financial activity;
* distinguish consumption from financial settlement;
* understand shared financial situations without losing individual economic ownership;
* compare actual behavior with financial goals and budgets;
* make better financial decisions;
* follow progress toward financial objectives.

## Product Principles

### Financial Reality First

The system should represent what economically happened rather than merely recording cash movements.

### Shared Does Not Mean Owned

A shared financial context must not automatically imply shared ownership of income, assets, or expenses.

### Historical Facts vs Forecasts

Historical financial facts must remain distinguishable from estimates, forecasts, and future commitments.

### Evidence Matters

Documents can provide evidence of financial events, but the existence of an expense must not depend on having a document.

### Analysis Requires Structured Data

Financial events should preserve enough structured information to support future analysis without introducing unnecessary complexity prematurely.

## Product Evolution

The product will evolve incrementally.

Future capabilities may emerge from validated user needs, including richer purchase, price, promotion, investment, budgeting, and analytical capabilities.

Capabilities must not be declared out of scope merely because they are not part of the current Feature. Their roadmap placement should be decided explicitly when appropriate.
